Our work

Build local capability.
Solve local problems.

The Kampala Hub exists to help young people move from learning about AI to using it responsibly on challenges that matter in our communities.

Our direction

Grassroots innovation · AI talent · Responsible AI · SDG impact

What we are trying to change

AI should not arrive as a finished answer to problems defined somewhere else.

We want more young people in Kampala to understand the technology, identify meaningful problems around them, work with affected communities and build solutions that are technically useful, socially grounded and responsible.

Three pillars

From skills to community impact.

Our work follows the same broad spirit as AI for Good: practical solutions, stronger capability and responsible governance.

01

Grassroots innovation

Start with real community needs in areas such as health, agriculture, education, climate resilience, accessibility and public services. Define the problem before choosing the AI.

02

Build AI talent

Grow practical capability through workshops, peer learning, mentorship, research conversations, small team projects and exposure to the wider AI for Good network.

03

Responsible AI

Make fairness, safety, inclusion, privacy, data quality and human accountability part of the work from the beginning, not an afterthought.

AI for the SDGs

Every project should answer: who benefits, and what changes?

The Young AI Leaders community tracks impact against the Sustainable Development Goals. Kampala should do the same, using the SDGs as a practical lens for problem selection and measurement.

SDG 3

Good Health

Health information, prevention, diagnostics support and responsible use of health data.

SDG 4

Quality Education

AI literacy, skills development, better learning tools and inclusive access to knowledge.

SDG 8

Decent Work

Talent pathways, entrepreneurship and tools that strengthen productive local opportunities.

SDG 9

Industry & Innovation

Local research, experimentation and useful prototypes built around Uganda's needs.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Local languages, accessibility, inclusive datasets and broader participation in AI.

SDG 13

Climate Action

Climate resilience, agriculture, resource use and data-informed adaptation.

How an idea moves

Problem → team → prototype → evidence → impact.

We do not want activity for activity's sake. The aim is to turn learning into useful work.

1

Listen

Understand the people, context and problem before proposing technology.

2

Frame

Define users, available data, risks, constraints and a realistic measure of success.

3

Build

Form a small multidisciplinary team and create the smallest useful prototype or research output.

4

Test responsibly

Check usefulness, safety, bias, privacy, accessibility and whether the solution actually fits the context.

5

Share and improve

Document the work, invite critique, connect to partners and contribute learning back to the community.

Connected to AI for Good

Kampala is local by design, global by connection.

AI for Good gives Young AI Leaders access to a wider platform for mentoring, collaboration, training, international events and opportunities to showcase projects. The Kampala Hub should translate that global access into stronger local work.
