Data & language

Local data and local languages are infrastructure.

If communities are missing from datasets, they can also become missing from the benefits of AI.

AI systems inherit the strengths and blind spots of the data used to build them. When local languages, informal work, rural realities and underrepresented communities are absent, the resulting systems can misunderstand, exclude or simply ignore important parts of society.

Building local data capacity is therefore not a side project. It is part of the infrastructure required for useful and inclusive AI. That includes careful collection, documentation, consent, quality control, language resources and responsible sharing.

The goal is not to collect data for its own sake. It is to make sure local knowledge is represented in the systems that increasingly shape decisions, services and opportunities.
