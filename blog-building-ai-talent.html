Talent

Building AI talent means creating pathways to practice.

People learn faster when skills are connected to projects, mentors, feedback and visible opportunities to contribute.

A workshop can introduce a tool. It rarely creates confidence on its own. Real capability grows when people repeatedly apply what they are learning to meaningful problems, receive feedback and see how their work connects to a wider community.

For the Kampala Hub, talent development should therefore include project clinics, peer learning, research conversations, mentorship, showcases and opportunities to collaborate across disciplines.

The goal is not only to produce more people who can use AI tools. It is to help develop young people who can ask better questions, make responsible technical choices, work with communities and turn learning into useful outcomes.
