Why Kampala

Local experience belongs in the global AI conversation.

Our hub is a place for practical learning, honest questions, shared experiments and collaboration. We connect Kampala's talent and priorities to an international community while keeping responsible, inclusive impact at the centre.

AI for the SDGs

Local innovation should move the Sustainable Development Goals forward.

Our work is guided by the idea that AI should help communities solve real problems responsibly, inclusively and with measurable social value.

SDG 3 · Good Health

Explore responsible AI and data approaches that can strengthen access, prevention and health decision-making.

SDG 4 · Quality Education

Grow practical AI literacy, technical capability and pathways for young people to participate in the AI economy.

SDG 8 · Decent Work

Build talent, entrepreneurship and locally relevant AI solutions that can create productive opportunities.

SDG 9 · Industry & Innovation

Support grassroots experimentation, research and prototypes that respond to Uganda's own needs.

SDG 10 · Reduced Inequalities

Promote inclusive datasets, local-language technology and participation by communities too often left out.

SDG 13 · Climate Action

Encourage data-informed solutions for agriculture, resilience and climate adaptation.
