Why Kampala

We should not only consume AI. We should help decide what it becomes here.

Uganda has its own priorities, constraints, languages, institutions, communities and opportunities. The Kampala Hub exists to make sure young people can participate in shaping AI around that reality — not simply import finished ideas from elsewhere.

Our purpose

Three things should happen because this community exists.

Community is useful when it changes capability, creates stronger work and opens meaningful opportunities for people to contribute.

01

More people can build

Develop practical AI, data, research and product capability through peer learning, mentorship and real projects.

02

More local problems get worked on

Help small teams turn community challenges into careful research, prototypes, datasets, tools or public learning.

03

AI is used more responsibly

Make fairness, safety, privacy, inclusion, data quality and human accountability part of how we work from the start.
