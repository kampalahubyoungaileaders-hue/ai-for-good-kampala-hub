Why Kampala

Local communities should help define what AI is for.

We do not want to be passive consumers of systems designed around other contexts. We want young people in Kampala to understand AI deeply enough to question it, adapt it, build with it and decide when it is or is not the right tool for a problem.

The Hub connects that local work to the wider AI for Good network, creating pathways to global learning, collaboration, mentorship, standards conversations and opportunities.

What the Hub is here to do

Talent, grassroots innovation and responsible AI.

Everything we organise should strengthen at least one of these areas and ideally connect all three.

Develop AI talent

Practical technical learning, research conversations, mentorship, collaboration and opportunities for young people to grow from curiosity into capability.

Support grassroots innovation

Help members identify problems around them, work with affected communities and build useful AI-enabled solutions, research or public-interest tools.

Promote responsible AI

Bring fairness, safety, privacy, inclusion, accountability and human oversight into projects from the beginning.

AI for the Sustainable Development Goals

Local AI should contribute to outcomes people can actually feel.

AI for Good is grounded in the SDGs. Kampala Hub projects should be able to explain the problem being addressed, who benefits, the risks involved and which SDG outcomes the work aims to advance.

SDG 3

Good Health & Well-being

Responsible health data, prevention, accessibility and decision-support tools.

SDG 4

Quality Education

AI literacy, skills, accessible learning and stronger pathways for young people.

SDG 8

Decent Work & Growth

Talent pathways, entrepreneurship and technology that expands productive opportunity.

SDG 9

Industry, Innovation & Infrastructure

Locally relevant research, prototypes and infrastructure-aware innovation.

SDG 10

Reduced Inequalities

Local languages, accessibility, inclusive datasets and broader participation in AI.

SDG 13

Climate Action

Climate resilience, agriculture, adaptation and data-informed local action.
